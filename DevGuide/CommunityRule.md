# Ciallo - Community Rules

We follow general public discussion rules with one extra rule:

We strictly forbid any forms of political topics, including but not limited to patriotism, DEI, race, religion, national wars, and any other topics that separate people into groups to raise conflicts.

The only exception is you (or our community) are being persecuted substantively by some political groups and needing help.

Our community welcomes all friendly advanced intelligences interested in digital painting, both intelligent enough AIs and organisms (human or alien).
We tend to stay ignorant of your gender/nationality/religion/race, or any other group identities.
